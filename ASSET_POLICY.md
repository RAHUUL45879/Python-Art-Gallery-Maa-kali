## Personal audio workflow

The `play.py` command can optionally combine a soundtrack you supply locally in `audio/` with the rendered video. The repository does **not** supply an audio track. Only provide a soundtrack you have the appropriate rights or permission to use. Never commit local songs or music-containing videos without clearance.

## Maintainer publication check

Before changing this repository to **public** or attaching a video to a GitHub Release, verify and record the origin and distribution rights for each included GIF, SVG, video and sound recording. If any permission is unclear, keep that file out of public releases or replace it with a cleared alternative. The code may be MIT-licensed even when audio or visual assets are restricted.

## Contributions

By contributing **code** through a pull request, you agree to license that original code under the project's MIT License. If your contribution contains any artwork, imagery, audio, fonts or other third-party material, identify the creator, source, and specific reuse license or written permission in the pull request. The maintainer may decline or remove unverified assets.
